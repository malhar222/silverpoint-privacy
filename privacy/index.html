Silverpoint Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Silverpoint ("the app") is a sponsorship CRM for independent creators, made by Meshal Alharbi ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Silverpoint has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code or SDKs. We don't run any servers that receive your data.

Your deals and details

  • Your deals (brands and their contact details, amounts and their currencies, payments, dates, notes) and "Your Details" (name, email, address, payment instructions, tax details) are stored on your device.
  • If iCloud is turned on for Silverpoint, this information is also synced through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database and iCloud key-value storage), so it's available on your other devices. It's stored under your Apple ID, and we can't access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Invoices and exports

PDF invoices and CSV exports are created on your device. They're only shared when you choose to share them (for example by email or AirDrop), and from then on they're handled by the app or service you chose.

Reminders and calendar

Deadline reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. "Add to Calendar" opens Apple's calendar editor: Silverpoint can't read your calendars, and an event is only added if you confirm it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment information. The app checks your subscription status with Apple to unlock Premium features.

Children

Silverpoint isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children (or from anyone).

Deleting your data

You can delete individual deals in the app. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To remove data synced to iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and delete Silverpoint's data from your iCloud storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: silverpointapp@icloud.com
