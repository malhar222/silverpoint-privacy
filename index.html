Questions

Is Silverpoint free?

Yes, you can track up to 3 active deals for free. Silverpoint Premium unlocks unlimited deals, PDF invoices, payment reminders, earnings insights, and CSV export. It's $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year in the US, and the yearly plan starts with a 1-week free trial.

Who is it for?

Independent creators who work with brands: YouTubers, TikTokers, Instagram creators, podcasters, and UGC creators who'd rather not track sponsorships in a spreadsheet or their notes app.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and add your first deal.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on sync. Silverpoint has no servers, analytics, or tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Does it work with other currencies and VAT?

Yes. Each deal keeps its own currency, and invoices can include VAT, GST, or other tax with your tax number and the brand's.

Is there an Android version?

No, Silverpoint is for iPhone.
